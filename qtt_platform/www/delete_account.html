Delete your QTT OS account

Queen Touch Technology

In the app (quickest): open QTT OS › Account › Delete my account.
Or here: enter the email you sign in with. If it has an account, we email you a link — tap it to confirm.

What happens

  • Your account is deleted {{ grace_days }} days after you confirm. Until then you can sign in and tap Keep my account.
  • Your name, email, phone number, photo, sign-ins and devices are removed. Records you made in a business (for example invoices) stay with that business, without your name.
  • A business you own alone closes with your account: its subscriptions stop and nothing more is billed. If other people work in a business you own, remove them first (Business › Team).
  • Records we must keep by law (such as tax invoices) are kept for as long as the law requires.

See our Privacy Policy. Questions: info@queentouchtech.com.
