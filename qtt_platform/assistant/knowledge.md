# QTT OS — what the assistant knows

QTT OS is Queen Touch Technology's one app for running a small business: one
login, every product inside it, on Android, iOS, the web
(**queentouchtech.in/app** — the website **queentouchtech.in** explains every
app and service, with prices) and computers. A business (called a workspace)
subscribes to the products it needs; each person in it gets access to some
products, with a role in each. **Team** (the business's own chat) is free for
everyone in every business — no plan needed.

## The app itself (every user)
- **Apps (home)**: greeting, what needs you today (the "For you" cards), your
  apps, and one search box for everything.
- **Search**: finds customers, people, items and app screens. Understands
  typos, any word order, phone numbers, and everyday words ("salary" opens
  Office Pulse, "bill" opens Orbitra). Remembers recent searches.
- **Lists** (customers, people, items, invoices, bills, leave, claims,
  payslips, payroll, approvals, money in and out, requests, payments): the
  first 20 show, and more load by themselves as you scroll — no "Show more"
  button; "All N shown" at the end; pull down to refresh. Views and order are
  behind the filter button (e.g. Working / Left, Active / Archived, Unpaid /
  Paid, Sort by); whatever isn't the usual view shows as a chip under the
  search or the list's title — × removes it.
- **Customers**: one customer list per business, shared by all apps (Orbitra
  bills them, Onbox saves chat contacts into it). Individual or business
  (GSTIN, billing address), tags and notes; call, WhatsApp or email them;
  archive instead of delete. **Where it is**: with Orbitra (and a role that
  can use Customers) it's Orbitra › Customers — the same list, where a
  customer also shows what they owe and bought and New invoice; there is no
  separate Customers menu item then. Without Orbitra (e.g. Onbox only), or if
  the owner switched Customers off for your Orbitra role, it's Customers in
  the menu. Search, then the filter button: Active / Archived and tags; a
  filter that's on shows as a chip under the search (× removes it).
- **Team** (everyone, free — no plan needed): your business's own chat (like
  Slack or WhatsApp groups, but private to your business and inside QTT OS), a
  tab on the phone and in the side menu. Nothing to buy; only the AI extras use
  AI credits.
  - **Chats**: **#general** has everyone in the business (people join it when
    they join the business, and leave it when suspended or removed). **New
    message** → **New channel** (open to everyone in the business — anyone can
    read it and joins by writing — or **Private**, only the people added) or a
    person for a **direct message** (only people in the same business).
  - **Writing**: *bold* _italic_ ~strike~ `code` (WhatsApp-style marks, or the
    **Aa** button), links, **@Name** to mention someone (they're notified even
    if they muted the chat) and **@everyone** in a channel. **⊕** sends a photo
    or file (up to 25 MB; only people in that chat can open it); the **mic**
    records a **voice note** (up to 5 minutes). Ctrl+Enter sends on a computer.
  - **On a message** (hover on a computer, long-press on a phone): react with
    any emoji (tap a reaction to add yours or take it back), **Reply in
    thread**, **Quote in reply**, **Copy text**, **Edit** or **Delete** your own
    (shows "edited"; deleting removes it for everyone), **Pin to chat** (pinned
    ones are in Details), **Save for later** (with a reminder), **Make a task**.
  - **Threads**: a reply thread under a message keeps a topic together; the
    chat shows "N replies". The writer and everyone who replies get
    notifications for new replies. On a wide screen a thread opens beside the
    chat. **Threads** (in the hub) lists yours with new replies.
  - **Live**: messages, edits and reactions arrive at once; "Ravi is typing";
    under your latest message **Sent** / **Seen** (a direct message) or **Seen
    by N** (a channel). A **New** line marks where you left off.
  - **Presence and status**: a green dot = online now, amber = away (in the
    app in the last 15 minutes), none = offline ("Active 2 h ago"). Tap
    yourself at the top of Team to set a **status** (📅 In a meeting, 🚗 On the
    way, 🏗 At the site, 🍽 Lunch break, 🏠 Working from home, 🎯 Focusing, 🤒 Off
    sick, 🌴 On leave, or your own words and emoji) that clears after 30
    minutes … this week, or stays. People on approved leave today (Office
    Pulse) show "🌴 On leave today" by themselves. **Do not disturb** (30
    minutes to a week) holds all Team phone notifications until it ends (a
    moon on your photo).
  - **Notifications**: a phone notification for each new message in your chats
    (tap it to open that chat or thread). In a chat's **Details** switch off
    "Notify me of every message" to hear only when someone @mentions you.
  - **The hub**: **Activity** — @mentions, replies in your threads and
    reactions to your messages (last 30 days; a count shows what's new).
    **Saved** — messages you kept, with reminders (20 minutes, 1 or 3 hours,
    tonight, tomorrow morning, next week; a phone notification then). **Tasks** —
    tasks made from messages (For me, I gave, Done): what, who (they're
    notified), by when; tick when done.
  - **Search**: the 🔍 in Team (or in a chat for just that chat) finds messages
    in every chat you can see: every word must be in the message; filter by
    who wrote it, photos, files, voice notes or links; tap a result to jump to
    it.
  - **AI extras (AI credits)**: **✨ Catch me up** (in a chat, or the banner
    when 5+ messages are new) sums up what happened, what was decided, what's
    asked of you and the to-dos — 1 AI credit. **✨ Fill with AI** in Make a
    task suggests the task, the person and the date from the message — 1 AI
    credit; check it before making it. They use your business's AI credits
    (from your apps' plans); the summary only says what the messages say.
  - **Wide screens** (tablet, computer, the web app): the chat list on the left,
    the chat on the right, a thread beside it.
  - **Calls** (free for every business): 📞 voice or 🎥 video in any chat's
    header. A direct message rings the other person (a phone notification
    too — tap to join); a channel shows "call started" to its people; a green
    bar in the chat shows a call going on — tap to join. In the call: mute,
    camera, switch camera and speaker (phones), Leave; everyone's camera or
    photo in a grid, whoever speaks gets a green ring. When it ends the chat
    shows "📞 Call · 12 min · who was in it" (or "missed"). Calls go through
    QTT's own server (meet.queentouchtech.in), up to 50 people.
  - **With Office Pulse — the Workplace** (Team gets more for businesses with
    Office Pulse): **Share your screen** in a call (from a computer, the web
    app or Android), **Control a computer** (remote control, below), **Files**
    and **Remote help** in Team's hub (also on Office Pulse's home).
  - People in another business never see your chats.

## Office Pulse Workplace — Files, documents, remote control (with Office Pulse)
- **Files** (Team › Files or Office Pulse › Files): the business's files on
  QTT's own cloud, in the app. Spaces: **Team files** (everyone in the
  business reads and writes; 10 GB per business), **My files** (each person's
  own; 2 GB), **Company** (policies and forms — everyone reads; owners,
  admins and HR write), **HR** (owners, admins and HR only) and **From HR**
  (your own documents HR keeps for you, read-only). **+ New**: Upload a file,
  New folder, New document / sheet / presentation. ⋮ on a file: Send to a
  chat (a copy in a Team chat), Rename, Delete (kept in the trash 30 days —
  QTT can bring it back). The bar on top shows how much space is used.
- **Documents**: tap a Word, Excel, PowerPoint or PDF to open it in the
  editor (OnlyOffice, on QTT's own server): it saves as you type, and
  everyone who has it open edits together (you see their cursors). On a
  phone it opens in the app; on a computer or the web app, in a new tab.
  For now the editor shows the OnlyOffice logo, and up to 20 people can be
  editing at the same moment across QTT.
- **Employee documents** (Office Pulse › Manage › Employee documents, HR /
  owners / admins): pick a person → their folder (offer letter, ID proofs,
  contracts, letters). The employee sees only theirs, read-only, in Files ›
  From HR. **Company files** (Manage › Company files) opens Company.
- **Remote help** (Team › Remote help or Office Pulse › Remote help): see and
  control a computer as if sitting at it, through QTT's own server
  (RustDesk; remote.queentouchtech.in). The person at the computer sees it
  and can stop it any time.
  - Get help on this computer: 1) install the free **RustDesk** app
    (rustdesk.com), 2) **Copy server settings** and import them in RustDesk
    (⋮ › Network › ID/Relay server › Import), 3) **Ask for help in a chat**
    with your RustDesk ID (and its one-time password, which changes after
    each session) — the helper taps **Connect** on that message.
  - **Company computers**: owners, admins and HR Admins add the business's
    computers (name, RustDesk ID, an optional permanent password for one-tap
    help when nobody is at it — kept encrypted, used only by them, who uses
    it) and connect in one tap; everyone else sees the computers they use.
  - **Connect to an ID** someone reads out; or in a call, **Control a
    computer** (the others' Company computers, or their ID).
  - Connect opens the RustDesk app on your device (computer or phone).
- **Business** (owner / admin): Profile (logo, address, GSTIN, phone, bank,
  UPI — printed on invoices, payslips, PDFs); Team (invite by email, choose
  each person's apps and role, remove); Roles and access (below); **Plans and
  billing** (each app's plan and trial end, Explore QTT OS to start another
  app or service, and every invoice from QTT — apps and managed services
  together, unpaid ones first, with **Pay now** for the owner and admins;
  everyone can see them).
- **Invoices from QTT**: when QTT gives your business an app or a service, it
  starts at once and an invoice comes, due in 7 days (Home shows "An invoice to
  pay"). Pay with Pay now (UPI, card, net banking). The next one comes 3 days
  before the paid time ends; unpaid 7 days after that, the app or service
  pauses — paying turns it straight back on. Paid another way (cash, bank
  transfer)? Tell QTT; they mark it paid.
- **Roles and access** (Business › Roles and access, owner / admin): one table
  per app the business has — the areas down the side, the roles across, a tick
  where that role can use it. In Orbitra the owner taps a cell to let a
  Manager or Cashier use an area or not (Orbitra Admin always has everything;
  Settings stays the admin's; editing items and stock also turns on seeing
  them); Reset puts a role back to the usual. Office Pulse, Onbox and Managed
  Services roles are fixed and shown for reference. Changes apply at once —
  the person's app updates and the server checks every request.
- **Business knowledge** (Business › Knowledge, owner / admin): one place for
  what the business knows — what it does, who its customers are, what makes it
  different, hours, holidays, map link, delivery, payments, bookings, policies,
  products and services (with prices), questions and answers and team notes.
  Each Q&A is for Customers, Team only or Everyone; each team note is for
  everyone or some roles (picked from the business's roles). The WhatsApp
  receptionist answers customers from it (never team-only Q&A or team notes),
  the Marketing assistant writes posts, articles and newsletters from it, and
  the team's Know your business answers staff from it (only the notes for
  their role; never salaries or private HR details). The receptionist page
  edits the same facts and Q&A. The brand kit (main, second, accent, dark and
  light colours, heading and body fonts) plus the logo from Business › Profile
  is what every post uses — they never change in posts. The bar at the top
  shows how complete it is and the next things to add (at least 3 products or
  services, 5 Q&A, hours, delivery or bookings, payments…). "Also learn from"
  lets it use the QTT website, the online store's products and Orbitra items.
- **Type of business** (Business › Knowledge, top of About the business): pick
  from the list (food, shops, beauty and health, services, Technology — IT
  services, software and apps, digital agencies, tech training — hotels and
  homes, education and industry, church / temple, other). It picks the photos,
  post designs and ideas the Marketing assistant offers.
- **Photos for your posts** (Business › Knowledge › Photos for your posts,
  owner / admin): add your own photos — products, people (you, your team),
  your place — and mark what each shows. Post designs use them before any
  sample photo: people for the people designs, products for the product
  ones. **Remove background** cuts the person or product out of its photo
  (on QTT's own server, about 10–15 seconds; 20 a day) so it sits right on
  the design without a frame, like a poster; switch "Without background" off
  to use the photo as it is. JPG, PNG or WEBP up to 10 MB, 60 photos.
- **Brand kit fonts**: pick the heading and body font from the list (Google
  fonts that also cover Tamil, Hindi and Arabic); Default uses Poppins and
  Inter. **See your brand** draws the saved brand kit as one picture: your
  logo, your colours (yours marked "yours"; any you left empty are worked out
  from your main colour), the fonts, and every background pattern and element
  posts are made from — all in your colours. It is drawn on QTT's own server
  from designs, never by an AI image service. Save first to see changes.
- **Test lab** (Business › Knowledge › Test lab, owner / admin): try the
  knowledge before anyone sees it — as a customer (the WhatsApp receptionist's
  own answer when the business has one, including when it would hand over to
  the team), as the team (pick a role to see what that role is told), or
  "Suggest this week's ideas" for posts (only from the knowledge — no made-up
  offers, prices or dates; copy a caption). Nothing is sent or kept; 60 tries
  a day.
- **Your team's questions** (Business › Knowledge): what the team asked Know
  your business that the knowledge couldn't answer, newest first. Add answer
  puts it into the Q&A (Team only, Customers or Everyone) so the next person
  gets it; Dismiss removes it from the list.
- **Know your business** (everyone in a business: the app's menu › Know your
  business, or Account › Help): team members ask how things work here —
  products and prices, hours, delivery, policies, how things are done. It
  answers only from Business › Knowledge: the customer facts, Q&A for the team
  or everyone, and the team notes for the person's role (owner and admins: all
  notes). It never answers pay, payslips, bank, ID or anyone's personal
  details (ask HR or the owner), never invents, and can't change or send
  anything. If it doesn't know, it says so and the owner sees the question.
  Team members can also ask on WhatsApp: message QTT's WhatsApp number from the
  phone number on their account or employee record — the owner can switch this
  off in Business › Knowledge ("Your team can also ask on WhatsApp"). An owner
  with Ask your business on gets their business numbers and the knowledge.
  60 questions a person a day.
- Each person has one role per app (they can have different roles in
  different apps). To give someone more, change their role in Business › Team,
  or (Orbitra) change what their role can do in Roles and access.
- **What a role can do in this business**: the person's details include
  "Roles and access in this business" — that is the truth for this business,
  with the owner's changes. Answer from it (name the role and what it can and
  can't do); the role lists below are the usual defaults.
- **Account**: switch between several accounts, appearance (light / dark,
  theme, text size, density), language (English, Tamil, Hindi, Arabic and
  more), tips on/off, help and support (WhatsApp or email QTT), fingerprint /
  Face ID sign-in, sign out.
- **Signing in**: email and password, Continue with Google, or fingerprint /
  Face ID on a phone that signed in before. Two-step verification is set by
  the business admin. New businesses verify their email and WhatsApp number
  with a one-time code when signing up.
- **Invitations — why**: an invitation is the safe way to bring a person into
  a business. The owner never sets or knows anyone's password (the person
  chooses their own); the code goes to that email, so it proves the email is
  theirs; it never touches someone's existing QTT account (it only adds this
  business to it); the app and role are chosen up front, so the person sees
  exactly what that role allows from the first minute; each code works once,
  expires in 7 days, can be revoked, and every join is in the audit log. One
  person can join several businesses and switch between them.
- **View as (business owner only, Business › Team › a person › View as
  <name>)**: see the app exactly as that person sees it — their apps, screens,
  numbers and what their role allows — to check what they see or help them.
  View only: nothing can be saved, sent, approved or changed as them (the
  server refuses it), and the Ask QTT assistant is off while viewing. A bar on
  top says "Viewing as <name>" with Exit; it ends by itself after 30 minutes
  without use, or when the person leaves the business. Admins can't use it,
  and QTT staff accounts can't be viewed. Every start and stop is recorded.
- **Inviting (owner / admin, Business › Team › Invite teammate)**: enter the
  email, pick the app and the role. The person gets an email with a **Join**
  button and a short code like **K7M2-QX9P** (8 letters and numbers, no 0/O or
  1/I/L). Pending invitations show their code; the copy button copies a
  message ready for WhatsApp or SMS (the tap-to-join link and the code) — use
  it if the email didn't arrive. Inviting the same email again sends a new
  code (the old one stops working). Revoke cancels an invitation. Office Pulse
  › People can also invite an employee ("Send invitation" / "Give access").
- **Joining (the invited person)**: tap **Join** in the email → a page shows
  the business and the code → **Open QTT OS and join** opens the app with the
  code filled in (no app yet: get it, then tap the button again). Or open the
  app and tap **I have an invitation code** on the sign-in screen (signed in
  already: tap your photo at the top right → **Join with code**, or
  on the choose-a-business screen) and type or paste the code — capitals,
  spaces and the dash don't matter; the Paste button picks the code out of a
  shared message.
  - New to QTT: enter your name and choose a password, then Join — you're
    signed in straight away.
  - Already use QTT with that email: sign in first (with that email), then
    Join with code. The code never sets a password for an existing account.
  - Problems: "invalid or has already been used" → ask for a new invitation;
    "expired" (after 7 days) → ask for a new one; "An account already exists…
    sign in with that account first" → sign in with the invited email, then
    enter the code again; too many wrong tries → wait 15 minutes.
  - After joining you have the app and role from the invitation; the business
    appears at once.
- **One switcher for everything** (like Google's apps): your photo at the
  right end of the top bar (the open business's logo sits on it as a small
  badge; the up/down arrows beside it mean there's another account or
  business to switch to). Tap it: the account that's open (Manage your
  account), **Your businesses** (tap one to switch; Join with code), **Your
  accounts** — your other signed-in accounts (tap one to switch — no
  password) — and Add another account. The menu (☰) can switch business too. Or swipe down / up on your photo to jump straight to
  the next / previous account. Signing out of one keeps the others. (Not on
  the web version: a browser keeps one sign-in per site.)

- **Delete my account** (Account › Security › Delete my account, or on the
  web at app.queentouchtech.in/delete_account — that one emails a link to
  confirm): it's deleted **7 days later**; until then "Keep my account" on
  the same row cancels it. Removed: name, email, phone, photo, sign-ins and
  devices; records made in a business stay with it, without the name. A
  business you own alone closes with it (subscriptions stop, nothing more
  billed). If other people work in a business you own, remove them first
  (Business › Team) — the app says so.

## Getting started (everyone)
- **Welcome**: the first time someone opens QTT OS they see what it does for
  them — each of their apps with what their role can do there, Ask QTT, Home
  cards and search, and (owners) what else QTT offers. Again any time:
  Account › Help › Show the welcome again.
- **Set-up guide** (HR, Office Pulse › Manage › Setup › Start here, and a "Set
  up Office Pulse: N of M done" card on Home until it's done): the steps in
  order, each saying why, ticked from the business's own data, with Next = the
  one to do now:
  - **Do these first, in order**: 1) choose your kind of business; 2) add
    your team (Excel import or one by one); 3) this year's holidays and
    week-off days; 4) create shifts (only the hours — making one doesn't give
    it to anyone); 5) decide who works which shift — a shift pattern if the
    week repeats with different days, or Assign if every day is the same;
    6) where people can check in (office location, or anywhere with a reason);
    7) give the team the app (invitations).
  - **Then**: leave types and giving leave for the year; payroll (HR Admin,
    plan with payroll): a salary template → each person's salary → run payroll
    monthly (check, fix, finalise, publish).
  - **Optional — only when you need it** (already working with sensible
    defaults): approval steps, late / check-out rules, departments and job
    titles, a biometric device, work sites, shift coverage, overtime pay.
  - **Shifts, assigning, patterns, one-day changes**: shift = hours; assign =
    give a shift to people from a date (or for a while); pattern = a repeating
    week or rotation that gives shifts by itself; one-day change = one person,
    one day (patterns keep it). Use a pattern or assigning per person, not
    both. Holidays and week-offs come from the holiday calendar, not shifts.
  - When someone asks "what should I do first / next?", answer from the guide's
    order and point to Setup › Start here.
- **Every app has a set-up guide** for the person who sets it up, with a
  "(guide): N of M done — Next: …" card on Home until the essentials are done,
  and a **Who can do what** section listing each role, what it can and can't
  use in this business (owner's changes included):
  - **Orbitra** — "Set up Orbitra" (Orbitra Admin; Orbitra › More › Start here): first — business
    details (address and logo; GSTIN only if registered — billing works
    without it), Orbitra settings (prices include GST or not, main store), add
    items, add opening stock, first bill at POS; then — invite the team, choose
    what each role can do, customers you give credit to; optional — supplier
    bills, more stores, reports.
  - **Onbox** — "Set up Onbox" (Manager; Onbox › Onbox settings › Start here): first — connect
    WhatsApp Business (Meta Cloud API: phone number ID, business account ID,
    access key; the number can't be in the WhatsApp app at the same time),
    website chat, invite the team, answer a first chat; then — Facebook,
    Instagram, email; optional — who sees which inbox.
  - **Managed Services** — "Get your website or store live" (Owner;
    Managed Services › Start here): choose what
    QTT builds → pay to start → check the preview and approve → go live on
    your own domain; for a store then add products and set payments and
    delivery; optional — ask QTT for a change, add another service.
- **One app instead of separate companies** (say this to owners when it
  helps): QTT OS does the jobs businesses usually pay different companies for
  — Office Pulse instead of separate HR software and attendance machines'
  software; Orbitra instead of separate billing software; Onbox instead of a
  separate WhatsApp inbox company; Managed Services instead of a web agency,
  hosting company and developer. One login, one team list, one place for
  help; each app has its own plan (Business › Plans) — start with one, add
  others when needed. Never name or compare other companies' products.

## Office Pulse — attendance, leave, expenses, payroll (HR)
Roles: **Employee** (own attendance, leave, expenses, payslips), **HR**
(everything except payroll), **HR Admin** (everything including payroll).
Anyone with people reporting to them is a **manager**: they see Team today and
approve their team's requests.
- **Check in / out** from the Office Pulse home. If offices are set, you must
  be within the office radius; if the business allows check-in away from the
  office, you add a reason and it is marked remote.
- **Work From Home / On Duty**: My attendance › "Request work from home / on
  duty" — choose dates, details; a manager or HR approves. It does not use
  leave.
- **Leave** (Office Pulse home › Leave): balances per leave type and your
  requests. **Apply for leave**: the leave type (with days left), the dates,
  Half day (one day only), a reason; it shows how many days of leave that is
  (holidays and weekly offs aren't counted) and goes to your approver.
  - **Past days**: by default leave starts today or later. HR can allow it to
    start up to N days ago (e.g. sick leave already taken) in Setup › Leave
    types › Leave for past days (0–90). The calendar only offers the days
    allowed; for older days, ask HR.
  - **Send is blocked** when the days are all holidays or weekly offs (no leave
    needed), or when they're more than is left of that leave type (choose
    fewer days or another type). Unpaid leave has no balance.
  - Other reasons a request is refused (shown on the screen): it overlaps
    leave you already asked for, it's before you joined, the month's payroll
    is already done, you were already marked present that day, the leave type
    allows fewer days in a row, or no leave of that type was given for those
    dates (HR gives it in Setup › Leave types › Give leave).
  - Withdraw: an open request, or approved leave that hasn't started yet; for
    the rest ask HR.
- **Expenses** (Office Pulse home › Expenses): your claims, newest first, with
  their status. **New expense claim** — one or more expenses (what for,
  amount, date, details) and receipt photos or PDFs (up to 5 MB each; a claim
  with receipts is approved faster), then Send claim. Tap a claim to open it:
  the total, its status, each expense, then a separate **Receipts** section
  (tap one to see the photo full size or save the PDF), and **Withdraw claim**
  while it's still pending and yours. The claim shows its status only, not
  who it's waiting for — ask your manager or HR.
  Statuses: **Pending** — waiting at an approval step (your manager, HR or a
  chosen person, per Setup › Approval flows; with several steps it moves on
  after each one); **Approved** — the full amount is approved; **Rejected**;
  **Paid** — the business has paid you. Approved claims are paid by the
  business, not in payroll; ask HR when. A decided claim can't be changed:
  make a new one.
- **Payslips** (Office Pulse home › Payslips): one per month, newest first,
  after HR runs payroll and finalises it (none yet = not finalised). Tap one:
  the period and **days paid** out of working days (days absent without paid
  leave aren't paid), **net pay** (also in words), each **earning** (from the
  salary template HR gave you — the starter one splits salary into Basic,
  House Rent Allowance and Special Allowance — plus any approved overtime or
  on-call pay) with gross pay, each **deduction** HR added to the template
  (e.g. PF or professional tax; the starter template has none) with the
  total, and **Download PDF**. Net pay =
  gross pay − total deductions. If a payslip looks wrong, ask HR — they fix it
  in Payroll before the next run.
- **Approvals** (managers, HR): leave, WFH / on duty and expense requests;
  approve or reject with a note.
- **People** (HR): add employees, give app access (email invitation linked to
  the employee), mark attendance, set salary, mark as left.
- **Setup** (HR): ask for location at check-in; allow check-in away from the
  office; offices (stand at the office and save its location; radius);
  leave types (yearly limit, unpaid, carry over) and giving leave; holiday
  calendars (holidays and week-off days — see Holidays below); shifts (start, end, late after minutes,
  automatic attendance from check-ins); departments and job titles.
- **Holidays** (HR, Setup › Holidays): a **calendar** is a year's named
  holidays (festivals, public holidays) plus its **week-off days** — one or
  more days of the week, e.g. Sunday only, Saturday and Sunday, or Friday.
  - **Who uses which**: the **whole company** uses one calendar; a **team**
    (department) can use another; a **person** can have their own. A person's
    own beats their team's, which beats the company's. Someone who moves team
    gets the new team's calendar (unless they have their own, which stays).
  - **Assign a calendar**: Setup › Holidays › Assign a calendar → choose Whole
    company, Teams or People → the calendar → tick the teams or people → "Use
    … for N". For teams or people choose "Back to the usual calendar" to undo.
    Each row shows what they use now and where it comes from (own / team /
    company).
  - **Different week-offs for a team or person**: make a calendar with those
    week-off days (New → name, year, tick the days, optionally "Start with the
    holidays of" another calendar so festivals are copied), then assign it to
    the team or people.
  - **Add a holiday**: open the calendar → Add holiday → pick the date → name
    it. In the date popup, days that already have a holiday are marked
    (tinted, with a dot; the month's holidays are listed below) and can't be
    picked — tapping one says which holiday it is. One holiday a day: to
    change a day's holiday, remove it (×) first, then add the new one.
    Week-off days show muted.
  - **Change week-off days**: open the calendar, tick the days, Save week-off
    days — every such day is marked off for the whole year. Named holidays
    stay; a holiday on a week-off day keeps its name; removing it makes the
    day a week-off again. At least one working day must remain.
  - **Next year / an ended calendar**: a calendar covers one year. When a
    calendar in use has ended, nobody using it has holidays or week-offs —
    Setup › Holidays shows a red warning ("… ended on …") and HR gets a "Your
    holiday calendar has ended" card. Tap **Make the {year} calendar**: it
    copies the holidays to this year with the same week-off days and moves
    everyone who used the old one (the company, teams, people) onto it. For
    next year ahead of time: New → next year → "Start with the holidays of"
    this year's, then assign it.
  - **For employees**: "Your week-off days" and the next holidays show on the
    Office Pulse home (Upcoming holidays); week-offs and holidays are marked
    on My attendance. They come from the calendar HR set for the company,
    their team or them — ask HR to change it.
  - Everything follows each person's calendar: their attendance calendar,
    leave day counts (holidays and week-offs aren't counted), automatic
    attendance from check-ins (not marked absent on their days off), shifts
    and coverage (a day off isn't a working day), the Office Pulse home, and
    Team today (each person shows as off by their own calendar).
- **Who works a shift pattern** (HR, Setup › Shift patterns › a pattern › Who):
  Everyone, a department, or chosen people. Chosen people are ticked right on
  the page (search, Choose all / Clear); each person shows the pattern
  they're on now ("on “Office Week”", "no pattern", "on this pattern"). A live
  summary shows who will really work it once saved: "N people will work this
  pattern", **Joining** (and from which pattern), **Stay on another pattern**
  (picked by name for another pattern, or their department's — picked by name
  beats a department, which beats Everyone; among equals the most recently
  saved pattern wins) and **Leaving this pattern** (and where they go). Save and
  apply asks first and says how many join, leave or stay elsewhere. To move
  someone off another pattern, pick them by name here (or untick them there).
  The patterns list says who each is for ("Department: Sales", "5 chosen
  people", "Everyone") and how many work it.
- **Giving a shift** (HR, Setup › Shifts › Assign on a shift): one page —
  **From** a date, and **No end date** or **Until a date** (a temporary change,
  e.g. a week of nights). Everyone shows their shift on that day: "No shift",
  "On Morning since 1 Mar", "Already on this shift" (can't be ticked), or
  "Shift pattern “…” decides their shifts". People on a pattern: a lasting
  change is made by editing the pattern (Setup › Shift patterns); a change
  **for a while** works — choose Until a date (up to 2 months) and their
  working days in those dates get the new shift as one-day changes (their
  days off and holidays stay), and the pattern carries on after; they're
  told once. "Everyone without a shift" ticks them all.
  **Move them from their current shift** (on by default): their current shift
  ends the day before; for a temporary change it stops for those dates and
  carries on after — off: people already on a shift are left as they are.
  HR's one-day changes are never overwritten. The button says exactly what
  happens ("Give Night to 5 people from 1 Jun 2026"); the result names anyone
  it didn't work for, with the reason. The Shifts list's "N people" is how
  many are on the shift today.
- **Giving leave** (HR, Setup › Leave types › Give leave on a type): one page —
  choose the year (this or next), the days for each person (it warns above
  the type's yearly limit), and who: everyone shows whether they already have
  this leave for that year ("Has 12 days · used 3") or "Not given yet";
  everyone without it is chosen to start; search, Choose all / Clear. People
  who already have it are left as they are, unless "Also change people who
  already have it" is on — then their days are set to the new number (it
  can't go below what they've already taken). The button says exactly what
  happens ("Give 12 days to 5 people"). After giving, a summary lists by
  name: given, changed, already had it (left as they were) and couldn't —
  with the reason, e.g. "more than 12 days a year are allowed for Casual
  Leave", "they joined after the start of this period", "they have already
  taken more days than that". To add days for someone who has it: turn on
  "Also change people who already have it" and choose just them.
- **Departments and job titles** (Setup › Departments and job titles): add
  them here (or with Add new on an employee's form, or by import); each
  shows how many people are in it. Put someone in one: People › the person ›
  Edit › Department. Departments are used for shift patterns (a pattern for
  a department) and holiday calendars (a team's own holidays). Job titles are shown on profiles and approval cards; they don't
  change what someone can do in the app — their role does (Business › Team,
  Roles and access).
- **People** (HR): search by name, phone or email; Working / Left; Add (one
  person), Import (Excel / CSV, many at once), Export. A person: Details ›
  Edit; **Give app access** (an email invitation — when they accept, their
  login is linked to them; or "they already have a login"); Attendance and
  Mark attendance for a day; **Shifts** — their next two weeks: tap a day for
  another shift, place or a day off (only that day; they're told; a shift
  pattern edit never undoes it; Back to the pattern undoes it); Salary (HR
  Admin); **Mark as left** on a date — their Office Pulse access is removed,
  records are kept, and they show under Left. Add / Edit: first name, gender
  and date of birth are required; joining date empty = today; Reports to =
  their manager (approves their requests, sees them in Team); Device PIN =
  their user ID on biometric devices. Everyone the app: Home's "give the team
  the app" card / Invite all.
- **Giving a shift, a salary template or a shift pattern to many people**:
  the result names who it worked for and who it didn't, each with the
  reason; nobody is left half-done.
- **Payroll** (HR Admin): 1) Salary templates with pay items (earnings and
  deductions; formulas like base * 0.4), 2) give a template to people with
  their monthly salary, 3) Run payroll for a month, check gross / deductions
  / net, then Finalise and publish payslips (employees then see them).
  - **Pay items** (Payroll › Pay items): name, Earning or Deduction, a short
    code used in formulas (e.g. BS), and an amount or a formula. Earnings add
    to gross pay; deductions (PF, professional tax, advances…) come off it:
    net = gross − deductions. Starter items: Basic Salary BS = base * 0.5,
    House Rent Allowance HRA = BS * 0.4, Special Allowance SA = base − BS −
    HRA (so the three add up to the monthly salary). Fixed amount: the same
    for everyone (e.g. a 200 professional tax); formula: follows each
    person's salary. To add PF: a Deduction pay item (e.g. PF = BS * 0.12),
    then add it to the template (a copy, if the template is in use).
  - **Salary templates** (Payroll › Salary templates): one per kind of job,
    not per person — each person's monthly salary is entered when the
    template is given to them, and formulas work it out. "base" = that
    monthly salary; a formula can use earlier items by short code. Save draft
    while working on it; **Start using** makes it final (it can then be given
    to people, and can't be edited — Make a copy to change it, then give the
    copy to people from a date). The list shows how many people are on each.
  - **A person's salary** (People › the person › Salary): their history
    (template, monthly salary, from date) and Set salary with a template from
    a date. A raise or a new template = set it again from the new date (the
    old one stays in the history); two can't start on the same date. Nothing
    to choose → make a template and Start using it first.
  - **A payroll run** (Payroll › a month): first the checks — punches not in
    attendance yet, biometric punches waiting for a person, undecided
    requests, approved overtime not in a run, devices quiet since the period
    ended — fix them and Refresh; then each slip: days paid of working days
    (days absent without paid leave aren't paid, nor days before joining or
    after leaving), gross, deductions, net, overtime and on-call added.
    **Finalise and publish payslips**: employees see them and the salary
    entry is posted to the accounts. Finalised payslips can't be changed in
    the app — check before finalising; for a correction afterwards ask QTT
    (Account › Help and support).

## Office Pulse — biometric devices (ADMS)
- Fingerprint / face devices with ADMS ("Cloud server", "Push") — ZKTeco, eSSL
  and similar — send punches to Office Pulse by themselves. On the device:
  Menu › Comm › Cloud Server Setting → server adms.queentouchtech.in, port 80,
  Domain name on, HTTPS off, Proxy off; check its date, time and time zone.
- Setup › Biometric devices (HR): Add device by its serial number (Menu ›
  System info, or the label). A device no business has added is not accepted;
  its punches stay on it until it's added. Online = in touch in the last 5
  minutes. Optional: a site (punches count as at that site), In / out from the
  device or by the shift rules, a clock correction.
- Each person's user ID on the device is their Device PIN (People › the
  person, an import column "Device PIN" / "Biometric ID" / "Enroll ID", or
  Link people on the devices screen — names on the device are suggested). A
  PIN is unique within the business. Punches for a PIN nobody has yet wait
  and are saved as soon as it's linked. "Get missed punches" asks the device
  to send the last days again (duplicates are skipped).
- Device punches follow the same rules as app check-ins: late check-in
  approval, early exit / overtime, and they count for attendance and payroll.
- Home cards for HR: punches waiting for people; a device not in touch for a
  day. If a device only takes an IP address or can't find the setting, QTT
  sets it up (Help and support).
- Which devices work: eSSL / ZKTeco with Cloud server (ADMS) — yes; other
  brands with ADMS / Push (built on ZKTeco) — yes; face, fingerprint, card or
  PIN — all come in the same way; several devices per business — yes (one
  Device PIN per person on all of them). Device that only takes an IP address,
  or that only works with PC software (eTimeTrackLite, ZKTime) — QTT sets it
  up / switches it to Cloud server. USB / pen-drive only, or one device shared
  by two businesses — not supported.
- Troubleshooting: Offline → power, network, server adms.queentouchtech.in
  port 80 HTTPS off (Online within a minute). Someone's punches missing →
  their user ID on the device must be their Device PIN; waiting punches are
  under "PINs not linked to anyone" — link and they're saved. Wrong times →
  device date / time / time zone (India GMT +5:30), or a clock correction
  here. Everyone shows Check-in → In / out "By the shift rules". "Added to
  another business" → send QTT the serial number. Device used before Office
  Pulse → add it; it sends what it still holds; Get missed punches = last 7
  days, duplicates skipped. Someone left → mark as left and delete them on the
  device so the PIN can be reused.
- Attendance waits for an offline device (Attendance rules › "Wait for
  offline biometric devices before marking absent", 48 h by default), so its
  punches aren't missed. A punch or an approved correction that arrives for a
  day already marked automatically re-marks that day; leave, HR's own marks
  and days already in a submitted payslip are not changed (the payroll run
  flags them). The same punch is never saved twice, and pressing again within
  2 minutes counts as one punch.
- Payroll › a run › "Before you finalise": punches not in attendance yet,
  biometric punches waiting for a person, undecided requests, approved
  overtime not in a run, devices not in touch since the period ended, and
  punches that arrived after the slips were submitted.
- **A device's page** (tap it in Setup › Biometric devices): name; In / out
  — From the device (people press Check-in / Check-out on it) or By the shift
  rules (they just punch; Office Pulse works out in and out — use this when
  everyone shows Check-in); clock correction in minutes, only when the
  device's clock is wrong and can't be fixed on it (e.g. -5 when it runs 5
  minutes fast; between -720 and 720); Accept punches from this device — off:
  the device's punches aren't taken, it keeps them and sends them again once
  it's back on; Save (leaving with changes asks: Save, Discard or Keep
  editing); Get missed punches; Remove this device — its punches stop coming
  in, the ones already here stay, and it can be added again by its serial
  number. Latest punches show what became of each: Saved (in attendance),
  Waiting (its PIN isn't linked to anyone — link it and it's saved), Already
  in (a duplicate, or pressed again within moments) or Problem (it couldn't
  be saved; the reason is shown under it).
- Where to find it: Setup › Biometric devices (device list, server address
  with copy buttons, add device, PINs not linked, a device's settings, Get
  missed punches, latest punches); People › the person › Edit › Device PIN;
  the import's Device PIN column; Approvals for late / early punches.

## Office Pulse — shifts and workforce features (HR sets up)
Every business sets its own rules. Each screen below has a "How to set up"
card with steps and suggestions for the business type.
- **Business type** (Setup › Business type): Office, Security / field
  service, Hospital / healthcare, Factory / manufacturing, Retail /
  restaurant, Other. Tap **Use** to switch on what that business usually
  needs and suggested rules; change anything later. Features (capabilities):
  Flexible cycles, Sites, Requests, Split shifts, Overtime pay, Coverage
  (with handover and on-call), Offline attendance. Presets: Office — none;
  Security — flexible cycles, sites, requests, overtime pay, coverage,
  offline; Hospital — flexible cycles, sites, requests, overtime pay,
  coverage; Factory — flexible cycles, requests, overtime pay, coverage;
  Retail — sites, requests, split shifts, overtime pay, coverage.
  - **Use replaces the attendance rules** with the type's suggested ones (late
    after minutes, what a refused late becomes, check-out reminder, missing
    check-out, and for every type except Office early exit and overtime
    approval): Office — late after 15 min, refused → half day, check-out
    reminder 30 min; Security — late after 10, refused → late mark only,
    reminder 15, early exit after 10, overtime after 30; Hospital — late after
    10, late mark only, reminder 30, early exit 15, overtime 30; Factory —
    late after 5, half day, reminder 15, early exit 5, overtime 15; Retail —
    late after 10, late mark only, reminder 20, early exit 10, overtime 30.
    Shifts, patterns, people and holidays are not touched. Changed your rules
    already? Switch capabilities on one by one instead of tapping Use, or
    re-check Setup › Attendance rules after.
  - **Switching a capability off** stops it working and hides its screens and
    options (e.g. Sites, on-call, requests); what was set up is kept and comes
    back when switched on. Overtime pay off: overtime isn't added to payroll.
  - Flexible cycles are only for rotations that don't repeat weekly (4-on /
    4-off, 2-2-3, 28-day) or rotate over up to 8 weeks; a normal week (even
    with different hours per day) is an ordinary shift pattern.
- **Shifts** (Setup › Shifts): start and end (an end before the start ends
  the next morning — night shifts work), late after minutes, a **break**
  (unpaid minutes after hours worked, or paid; a break they checked out for
  counts as taken), and with Split shifts the time off in the middle (e.g.
  11:00–22:00 off 15:00–18:00 — not counted as work, checking out for it is
  not an early exit). Two different shifts on one day, and flexi hours, are
  not supported.
- **Shift patterns** (Setup › Shift patterns): which shift on which day and
  where (office, from home, anywhere, or a site). Repeats every week, every 2–4
  weeks (rotation; up to 8 weeks with Flexible cycles), or with Flexible
  cycles every N days, 2–56 (4 on / 4 off = 8 days, 2-2-3 = 14, four crews =
  28). Without Flexible cycles a 4 on / 4 off rotation isn't possible —
  switch it on in Setup › Business type. In a pattern: tap a day for its
  shift (or Day off) and where; Who shows, before saving, who joins, who stays
  on another pattern and why (chosen people beat a department, a department
  beats everyone), and who leaves. "Stop using this pattern": its people move
  to the next pattern that fits them, or keep the shifts given under Shifts.
  A pattern showing 0 people: everyone it's for is on a more specific pattern
  (chosen people or their department), or its department has no one. Save
  and apply gives shifts 60 days ahead in the background (a notification comes
  when done) and keeps topping up. A one-day change for a person is in People
  › the person › Shifts and survives pattern edits.
- **Attendance rules — the options**: a late check-in not approved makes
  the day Half day, Absent or Late mark only. Still no check-out at day end:
  Ask for the time (they get a request to fill in the time they left — it
  goes to the approver), Half day, or Nothing. An early exit not approved:
  Half day, Absent or Mark only. "Forgotten check-out: keep the shift open
  for N hours" (1–24): a check-out this long after the shift ends still
  counts for it — night shifts need ~12. Overtime needs approval: approved
  minutes count for payroll (with Overtime pay, HR Admin sets the rate in
  Payroll › Overtime and on-call pay). "Wait for offline biometric devices
  before marking absent" (hours): a device without network still holds the
  punches, so nobody is marked absent before it sends them.
- **Attendance rules** (Setup › Attendance rules): late check-ins need
  approval (after N minutes; if not approved: half day, absent or late mark);
  check-out required with reminders and what happens without one; how many
  hours a forgotten check-out still counts (night shifts ~12); early exits and
  overtime need approval (Requests); offline check-ins accepted for N hours;
  morning coverage alert.
- **Requests** (My requests, when the business uses Requests): shift change
  (another shift, place or a day off), shift swap (same day or trading days;
  the colleague accepts first), early exit, overtime. Late check-ins and
  missed check-outs also appear there with who they wait for.
- **My requests, step by step (every employee):** Office Pulse home › the
  **My requests** tile. Tap **New request** (shown only when HR has switched
  on Requests in Setup › Business type; without it ask HR) and choose:
  - Shift change — the day, then another shift and place (office, from home,
    anywhere or a site), or tick **Day off**.
  - Shift swap — your day, the colleague, and their day. Same day: you take
    their shift and they take yours. Another day: you give them your day and
    take theirs. The colleague accepts first: they see "requests waiting for
    your approval" on their Office Pulse home and tap Approve. Then it goes
    to the approvers.
  - Early exit — the day and the time you'll leave (also asked automatically
    when you check out early, if the business needs approval for that).
  - Overtime — the day and the minutes (also asked automatically when you
    check out late, if the business needs approval for that).
  Add a reason (optional) and **Send for approval**. Each request shows its
  status and who it's waiting for now (the manager, HR or a chosen person,
  per Setup › Approval flows). Once approved, your shift for that day changes
  (or the minutes count for attendance and overtime pay). A sent request
  can't be withdrawn — ask the approver to reject it. Leave and work from
  home are not here: Leave and My attendance › Request work from home.
- **Approval flows** (Setup › Approval flows): per kind of request up to 5
  steps, decided in order — manager or HR, manager, manager's manager, HR,
  HR Admin or a chosen person. A rejection at any step ends it; approved at
  the last step = approved.
  - **Only when**: Always, or only above a number in the kind's own unit —
    Leave and Work from home / on duty: days ("Days above 3"); Expense:
    amount ("Amount above 5000"); Late check-in, Early exit, Overtime:
    minutes; Missed check-out, Shift change, Shift swap: always (they have no
    number). A request at or below the number skips that step. If no step
    applies, the default decides (the manager or HR). E.g. big claims: step 1
    Manager (always), step 2 HR Admin only when Amount above 5000.
  - **Add HR after N hours** (0 = never, up to 168): if nobody decides a step
    in that time, HR can decide it too (checked every hour) — for approvers
    who are away or on leave.
  - Nobody approves their own request; a step nobody fits (e.g. no manager
    set) goes to HR. A swap first needs the colleague's yes.
  - Editing a flow changes new requests only; requests already waiting keep
    the steps they started with. "Use the default" removes the steps (asks
    first). Leaving with unsaved changes asks: Save, Discard or Keep editing.
  Approvals: filter chips by kind (Leave, Expenses, Attendance, Shifts,
  Check-in & out), each kind with its own icon and colour on its cards; cards
  show the leave balance left, and with Coverage a warning when approving
  would leave a shift short.
- **Overtime and on-call pay** (Payroll › Overtime and on-call pay, HR Admin,
  Overtime pay feature): hourly rate = monthly salary ÷ days ÷ hours (or a
  fixed rate) × multiplier (1.5, 2); counted in blocks (e.g. 15 min); on-call
  allowance per day. Approved overtime and on-call days are added to the next
  payroll run (shown before the slips are made), each paid once; only people
  with a salary set are paid.
- **Coverage** (Manage › Coverage, Coverage feature): rules — at least N
  people on a shift (at a site) on chosen days; gaps for the next 2 weeks;
  HR gets gaps for the next 3 days at 7 am; approvers are warned. **Handover
  notes**: "Leave a handover note" on the check-in card; the next shift (same
  site, else the business) sees it on their card. **On call** (Manage › On
  call): add a person for some days or a rotation (people in order, days
  each, times round); "on call now" shows on Team today and their card.
- **Offline attendance** (Offline feature): with no network, Check in /
  out is saved on the phone (shown "on phone") and sent when back online or
  with "Send now". Wrong phone clocks are corrected; future or too-old
  punches (default 72 h) are refused; a punch is never saved twice.
- On the check-in card: today's shift (both parts of a split shift), the
  break rule, time worked, on-call, handover notes.

## Orbitra — billing, POS, stock, GST (shops and small businesses)
Roles (usual): **Cashier** (POS, sales, customers; sees stock), **Manager**
(adds purchases, inventory editing, reports), **Orbitra Admin** (everything,
including accounts and settings). Purchases, Accounts and Reports also need a

  returns); turn a quote into an invoice.
- **Inventory**: items and services with selling price, cost, GST rate,
  HSN/SAC, barcode, category, low-stock alert level; add stock, count stock,
  move stock between stores; hide an item from POS and new bills.
- **Purchases**: supplier bills bring stock in and track what you owe; pay a
  supplier; manage suppliers.
- **Customers** (in Orbitra): the business's one customer list (search,
  Active / Archived, tags); a customer shows what they owe and bought, their

  cash and bank; who owes you / whom you owe.
- **Reports**: for a period — sales by day, best sellers and customers, GST

- **Settings** (admin): prices include GST or not, main store, invoice note.
  Business details on invoices come from Business › Profile.

## Onbox — every customer conversation in one inbox
Roles: **Agent** (answers chats assigned to them or their channels),
**Supervisor** and **Manager** (see every channel; managers also manage
settings).
- Channels: WhatsApp Business (Meta Cloud API), website chat (paste a code on
  your website), Facebook, Instagram and email (forwarding or mailbox).
- Conversations: Mine / Unassigned / All; filter by status (open, pending,
  snoozed, resolved) and channel; reply or add a private note; attach files
  (up to 16 MB); assign to a person or team; snooze; labels; priority;
  resolve / reopen; save the contact as a customer.
- WhatsApp rule: free-form replies only within 24 hours of the customer's last
  message; after that you can only add private notes (or send an approved
